View All FAQs

How can I schedule a site visit?

You can schedule a site visit by filling out the contact form above, calling us, or chatting on WhatsApp. Our team will coordinate a convenient time for you.

Where are your projects located?

Our prime commercial and luxury residential projects are strategically located across Noida, Greater Noida, and prime growth corridors in Delhi NCR.

What is the booking process?

The booking process is smooth and transparent. Select your unit, submit KYC documents along with the booking amount, and receive verified allotment paperwork instantly.

Can NRIs invest in Bhutani projects?

Yes, NRIs can seamlessly invest using their NRE/NRO accounts following standard RBI real estate investment norms and FEMA regulations.

How will I receive project updates?

We provide regular construction milestone reports, site photo updates, and financial status directly via registered email and our customer portal.
